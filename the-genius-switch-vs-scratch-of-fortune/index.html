A closer look

Do you want a routine or a reveal?

Both offers can be approached as personal reflection. The difference is what you do first: listen to a repeatable audio series or move through a six-number interaction.

01 / THE GENIUS SWITCH

Made for repeat listening.

The Genius Switch is presented as an audio-based digital product. The seller describes instant access and a short daily listening routine. That gives you a simple format: choose a time, press play, and decide whether the material belongs in your routine.

This may suit someone who already uses guided audio, music, or meditation tracks. You do not need to book a session or wait for a personalized report. The tradeoff is that the experience depends on your willingness to listen consistently and make your own judgment about the seller’s claims.

The product’s neuroscience language needs care. Claims about neurologists, whole-brain activation, brain power, and transformation remain seller statements. Inner North Journal treats this as an optional listening practice, not a medical, psychological, financial, or life-results solution.

  • Best for: people who want a repeatable audio routine.
  • What to expect: an audio-first digital experience as described by the seller.
  • What to check: current price, access, refund rules, and any repeat billing.

02 / SCRATCH OF FORTUNE

Made for an interactive pause.

Scratch Of Fortune is presented as an interactive fortune-reading experience involving six numbers and Madame Tessia. Its appeal is the activity itself: you move through the reveal, then decide what the result brings up for you.

This may suit someone who wants a short, tactile prompt instead of a daily audio habit. The useful way to approach it is as entertainment and reflection. The numbers can help you notice a thought or question, but they do not establish what will happen next.

The official framing matters. Scratch Of Fortune is not a lottery-prediction tool and is not financial advice. Do not use the numbers to choose investments, place bets, or make other money decisions.

  • Best for: people who want an interactive reflection prompt.
  • What to expect: a six-number experience led by the Madame Tessia presentation.
  • What to check: current price, access, refund, support, and cancellation terms.

THE SIMPLE CHOICE

Choose the activity you will actually finish.

Choose The Genius Switch if you want a listening habit you can revisit. It is a good fit for someone who prefers to work with audio and form their own view over time.

Choose Scratch Of Fortune if you want a one-session interactive experience. It is closer to a symbolic prompt than a routine, prediction, or source of financial guidance.

You do not need to decide which offer is more powerful. Ask three practical questions instead: Do I want audio or an interactive reveal? Will I use it again after the first session? Have I read the current terms before I enter payment details?
